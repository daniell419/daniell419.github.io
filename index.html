I study spatial economics

I research spatial economics and econometrics, with a focus on urban and international economics. I work under the supervision of Professor Gabriel Kreindler.

I hold a B.A. and an M.A. in Economics from Universidad de los Andes, Colombia. Previously, I served as a consultant at the World Bank’s Development Economics Research Group (DECRG); an adjunct professor, teaching assistant, and pre-doctoral fellow at Universidad de los Andes; senior data scientist at the consulting firm Quantil; and intern at the Central Bank of Colombia.

Research

Working paper

Spillover Gridlock: Revisiting Interference in Difference-in-Differences

M.A. thesis. Documentos CEDE 2026-26, Universidad de los Andes

A framework for difference-in-differences when treatment reaches untreated neighbors. It separates treatment effects from spillover effects with an imputation estimator, and applies it to road paving in Mexico and nearby property values.

Software

R package

spill_imputation

An imputation-based difference-in-differences estimator for spillovers. It uses a subset of never-exposed, untreated units to recover untreated counterfactuals and aggregate dynamic effects (ATOT and ASEU), without imposing a pre-specified exposure function.

devtools::install_github("daniell419/spill_imputation")
library(spillimputation)
