I am a pre-doctoral fellow at Harvard University's Department of Economics, working under the supervision of Professor Gabriel Kreindler. My work focuses on Econometrics and Development Economics, with a special focus on spillovers and causal inference. I hold a B.A. and an M.A. in Economics from Universidad de los Andes, Colombia. 

Previously, I served as a Consultant at the World Bank's Development Economics Research Group (DECRG); an Adjunct Professor, Teaching Assistant, and pre-doctoral fellow at Universidad de los Andes; Senior Data Scientist at the consulting firm Quantil; and Intern at the Central Bank of Colombia.

Feel free to email me if you want to chat!
